Music Engeen v0.1
=================

Windows x64 VST3 music-bus processor.

Recommended use:
- Route drums, bass, synths, guitars, piano and instrumental FX to one MUSIC BUS.
- Keep lead and backing vocals outside this bus unless you intentionally want them processed.
- Insert Music Engeen on MUSIC BUS.

Main controls:
CLEAN  - reduces mud and rumble.
PUNCH  - transient impact.
GLUE   - bus compression.
WEIGHT - low-end mass and harmonic density.
SHINE  - clarity / high-frequency energy with fizz protection.
WIDTH  - stereo width while keeping low side energy restrained.
LOUD   - soft clipping / final density into a safety limiter.

Secondary:
SUB / TILT / MIX / OUTPUT

Buttons:
AUTO   - recalls the Golden starting point and enables adaptive protection.
GOLDEN - recalls the Golden starting point.
SAFE   - caps extreme low, width, punch, shine and loudness settings.
BYPASS - bypasses processing.

Golden start:
CLEAN 48 / PUNCH 46 / GLUE 50 / WEIGHT 58 / SHINE 38 / WIDTH 46 / LOUD 34
SUB 42 / TILT 50 / MIX 100 / OUTPUT 0 dB

Install:
Copy the whole "Music Engeen.vst3" bundle to:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

Then rescan VST3 plugins in REAPER.
